Silent Night + LED lights

**Overview **

For this assignment, I used a buzzer to play Silent Night on my LilyPad Arduino. I found the melody code in Robson Couto’s Arduino Songs collection on Github.
I also added LEDs that light up one after another as each note plays, and a switch to control the music.
The song was initially in the setup.To make the song repeat, I moved the music-playing instructions from setup() into loop().



## Tips
Check your connections before changing your code! My song kept stuttering because of the switch connection. 
I switched to INPUT_PULLUP( which I learned from the arduino toolkit, and googling what some of the reasons for my usual switch input did not work), and adjusted the code accordingly.
