My first plushy

**Description**

For this project, I made a blue felt plushie with three LEDs. I started by drawing and cutting out a paper prototype, then traced the shape onto felt. Before sewing the circuit into the plushie, I used alligator clips to test how the LEDs and switches worked together. Once I had tested the connections, I attached the components to the felt and added the face. I learned that planning where the electronics go is just as important as designing how the plushie looks.


## Tips
 Test every connection with alligator clips before sewing it down. It is much easier to move a clip than to remove conductive stitches when something does not light up.
