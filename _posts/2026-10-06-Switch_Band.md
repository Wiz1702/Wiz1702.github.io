Made a wristband that lights up! Getting the connections right took some patience.

**Let there be light!**

For this assignment, I made a light-up wristband using LEDs and a homemade switch. I started with a paper prototype to plan the design, then used alligator clips to test the circuit before putting everything together.

Closing the switch completes the circuit and turns on the LEDs. Opening it breaks the circuit and turns them off. This project helped me understand how sewing and electronics can work together.

## Tips

Test your circuit with alligator clips before attaching everything permanently. Check the positive and negative sides of your LEDs, and make sure the conductive parts of your switch touch when it is closed.
