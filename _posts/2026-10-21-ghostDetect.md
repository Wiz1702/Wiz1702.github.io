Three fireflies, three different patterns, and one Arduino program. We finally brought everything together!

Code it

For this assignment, I combined our previous Big Dipper, Flash Bulb, and Blue Ghost programs into one file. Each firefly has its own blinking pattern: the Big Dipper gradually gets brighter, the Flash Bulb flashes every 4 seconds, and the Blue Ghost randomly changes its brightness.

The challenge was making all three LEDs work at the same time using just one timer and one delay() statement. After putting everything together and testing it on my LilyPad USB Plus, all three worked!

Tips

My biggest tip is to test each LED separately before combining everything. Also, pay attention to your timing variables. Using one shared timer makes the code much easier to organize.
And then, just pay attention in class, it truly works out
